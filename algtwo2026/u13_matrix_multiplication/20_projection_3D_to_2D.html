Question

A video-game developer is hoping to apply an oblique projection to show a set of 3D points as a 2D image. The starting points are stored as rows in a 5 \(\times\) 3 matrix \(S\).

\[S=\begin{bmatrix} 1.3997 & -0.8803 & 0.5158\\ -0.4954 & -1.4275 & 0.8467\\ -0.9521 & -0.9939 & -1.0516\\ 0.943 & -0.4467 & -1.3825\\ -0.2238 & 0.9371 & 0.2679 \end{bmatrix}\]

The oblique projection matrix \(P\) has two key parameters: \(\theta\) and \(r\). For this problem, use \(\theta=0.8466\) and \(r=0.2642\).

\[P~=~\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ r\cdot\cos(\theta) & r\cdot\sin(\theta) \end{bmatrix}\]

The final points (in matrix \(F\)) are found by multiplying \(S\cdot P\).

\[F=S\cdot P\]


What is the new \(x\) coordinate when point \((-0.9521, -0.9939, -1.0516)\) is projected using the oblique projection with \(\theta=0.8466\) and \(r=0.2642\)?

Solution

Find the projection matrix by pluggin in \(\theta=0.8466\) and \(r=0.2642\).

\[P=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ 0.17504139816146 & 0.197894287258832 \end{bmatrix}\]

We use row 3 of the points matrix and column 1 of the projection matrix.

\[(-0.9521)(1)~+~(-0.9939)(0)~+~(-1.0516)(0.1750414)\]

Thus, \(x=-1.1361735\).
