Question

A video-game developer is hoping to apply an oblique projection to show a set of 3D points as a 2D image. The starting points are stored as rows in a 5 \(\times\) 3 matrix \(S\).

\[S=\begin{bmatrix} 0.9022 & 1.3629 & 0.5733\\ 1.353 & -0.5528 & 0.9294\\ 1.0172 & -0.9893 & -0.9933\\ 0.5664 & 0.9264 & -1.3494\\ -0.9597 & -0.1868 & 0.21 \end{bmatrix}\]

The oblique projection matrix \(P\) has two key parameters: \(\theta\) and \(r\). For this problem, use \(\theta=0.6292\) and \(r=0.3157\).

\[P~=~\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ r\cdot\cos(\theta) & r\cdot\sin(\theta) \end{bmatrix}\]

The final points (in matrix \(F\)) are found by multiplying \(S\cdot P\).

\[F=S\cdot P\]


What is the new \(x\) coordinate when point \((1.0172, -0.9893, -0.9933)\) is projected using the oblique projection with \(\theta=0.6292\) and \(r=0.3157\)?

Solution

Find the projection matrix by pluggin in \(\theta=0.6292\) and \(r=0.3157\).

\[P=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ 0.255242997128174 & 0.185788865158886 \end{bmatrix}\]

We use row 3 of the points matrix and column 1 of the projection matrix.

\[(1.0172)(1)~+~(-0.9893)(0)~+~(-0.9933)(0.255243)\]

Thus, \(x=0.7636671\).
