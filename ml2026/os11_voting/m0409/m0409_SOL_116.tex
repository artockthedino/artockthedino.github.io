\hypertarget{question-3}{%
\subsubsection{Question 3}\label{question-3}}

4 candidates (W through Z) are running in an election with a single
winner. There are 7 voters (A through G) who each rank the candidates
from 1 to 4, with 1 meaning first choice and 4 meaning last choice.
Those rankings are shown below. Each column represents a single voter's
ranking.

\includegraphics{m0409/m0409_SOL_116_files/figure-latex/unnamed-chunk-9-1.pdf}\\

A \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condorcet_method}{Condorcet
winner} would win in a head-to-head election against any other
candidate. Who is the Condorcet winner? (Or, possibly, there is none.)

\color{red}

Notice Y wins against all other candidates. Thus Y is the Condorcet
winner.

\color{black}
