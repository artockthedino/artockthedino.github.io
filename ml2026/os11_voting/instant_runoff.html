Question

In Adams, Massachusetts, 4 candidates (W through Z) are running in an election with a single winner. There are 11 voters (A through K) who each rank the candidates from 1 to 4, with 1 meaning first choice and 4 meaning last choice. Those rankings are shown below. Each column represents a single voter’s ranking.


Who would win an instant-runoff election?

Answerlist

  • Candidate W
  • Candidate X
  • Candidate Y
  • Candidate Z

Solution

First, there are 11 voters, so a candidate needs at least 6 votes to win a majority.

Nobody has a majority. Candidate Z has the fewest votes. Eliminate candidate Z from the contest. Each voter’s vote goes to their top candidate still in the election.

Nobody has a majority. Candidate Y has the fewest votes. Eliminate candidate Y from the contest. Each voter’s vote goes to their top candidate still in the election.

Candidate X has gotten majority vote. Candidate X wins!
