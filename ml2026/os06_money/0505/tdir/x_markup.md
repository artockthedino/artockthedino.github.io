

Question
========

A store's wholesale cost is $110 per trinket. To calculate the retail price, the store adds a markup percentage of 70%.

Find the retail price in dollars. (Do NOT include `$` in your answer.)


Solution
========

This scenario represents a percent increase. It is beneficial to find the scale factor.

$$\text{scale factor}~=~1+\frac{70}{100}~=~1.7$$

Then, multiply the original cost by the scale factor to find the retail price.

$$1.7\cdot 110 = 187$$

So the retail price is $187.
